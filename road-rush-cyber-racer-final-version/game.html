How To Play Road Rush

  • Use ← and → to steer.
  • Press A to accelerate.
  • Press D to brake.
  • Press SPACE to pause or resume.
  • Avoid enemy cars.
  • Collect shields, nitro and coins.
  • Answer all 5 cyber security questions.
  • Correct answer = +1 Life.
  • Wrong answer = -100 Coins.
  • Get at least 3 correct answers to unlock the next level.
